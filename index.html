I write the spec.
The build follows.
So does the release.

A year ago I had never written a line of code. I wanted things to exist, so I learned to specify them precisely enough that an AI coding tool could build them — and to own everything that comes after: the QA gates, the release checks, and the numbers that come back.

Twelve products later, the part I'm good at turned out not to be typing. It's knowing what should happen when the caption is empty, when the token expires, when the sample is too small to trust. Around a thousand commits in the past year, across twenty-three repositories.

I came to product from marketing and analytics — Aon, Kraft Heinz, Red Bull — and I read a funnel and a release checklist with the same eye. Boğaziçi University, 2027.
